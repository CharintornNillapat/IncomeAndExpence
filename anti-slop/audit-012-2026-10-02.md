# antislop audit 012: FinLife Tracker after Phase 63 (the identity colour migration), 2026-10-02

This is a mode 2 verification pass, run after the phase as in Phases 55 to 62. It covers:
- the shipped categories and starter wallets moving onto spec section 1's identity colours, on the device and (once applied) in the cloud;
- what that changes on the Dashboard (the category chart and the wallet bar), the Categories page (the colour grid) and the Wallets page (tiles and the colour picker);
- the copy changes: "Color" throughout, swatches named by colour, "Current color".

The direction is `docs/design/finlife-redesign-spec.md` (sections 1, 5.1 and 6.6) and `DESIGN.md`. Nothing in the findings table has been changed. **Pick the numbers you want fixed.**

## How it was checked

- **Code sweeps** of the Phase 63 diff (`ba87338..HEAD`, `src/`):
  - em dashes in added user-facing strings: 0;
  - raw palette classes: 0 (the new hex values are data colours, which are exempt);
  - `focus:outline-none`: 0;
  - `transition-colors` / `transition-all`: 0;
  - motion added: 0;
  - `font-mono`: 0.
- **Contrast.** No token changed. The identity colours were measured against the card surface (`surface-1`):
  - light: 1.9:1 (Aqua) to 3.0:1 (Blue);
  - dark: 5.8:1 to 8.9:1.

  The old colours they replace were no better in light: Transport & Fuel's yellow was 1.46:1.
- **Palette distances** (CIE76 ΔE, where under about 10 is hard to tell apart at a glance): Teal / Aqua 6.1, Lavender / Iris 9.8, Tan / Peach 13.2, Blue / Periwinkle 13.7.
- **Browser.** Playwright's own Chromium was driven by a script against the dev server, as a guest. Each run checked:
  - every inline colour on the page for any of the twelve old colours;
  - the colour grid's labels;
  - the wallet tiles and the edit form's swatches;
  - page overflow and controls under 44px;
  - console errors.

  Seeds and viewports:
  - **Seeds:** a device holding the old colours (the nine shipped categories, the three starter wallets and five transactions), and a fresh guest.
  - **Viewports:** 1280 light (both seeds), and 390 light and dark (the old seed).

  Results:
  - no old colour was painted at any width, on any of the three pages;
  - "Rose, used by Food & Dining", with seven of the twelve swatches struck;
  - Tan offered first;
  - the wallet tiles on Blue, Tan and Teal;
  - the swatches named "Tan" to "Iris" under "Color";
  - no overflow, no control under 44px and no console errors.
- **Tests.**
  - Lint is clean, and unit tests pass 578/578.
  - Playwright passed 398/402 and then 401/402. Every failure was a click timing out while waiting for an element to settle; see the refactor log.
  - The probe printed `PHASE 63 PROBE OK` against the live schema.

## Findings

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MEDIUM | C-2, human | **The colour rule now caps how many categories someone can add, and the cap arrives early.** L9 gives each Expense or Income category its own colour, from twelve. Before this phase the shipped categories sat outside the twelve, so all twelve were free. Now a fresh account has 7 in use and can add **5** categories. Your account, after the cloud migration, has 9 in use (the seven shipped plus camel and กิจนิมนต์) and can add **3**. After that, New category is disabled with "All 12 colors are in use, so a new category has none left." Nothing on the page says a 13th category is impossible until someone hits it. | `CategoryForm.tsx`, L9 |
| 2 | LOW | layout / human | **Two pairs in the palette are hard to tell apart at 12px.** Teal and Aqua are ΔE 6.1 apart, and Lavender and Iris 9.8. Transport & Fuel ships on Aqua and Shopping & Apparel on Lavender, so a category added on Teal or Iris looks like theirs in the chart and on the chips. The palette is the spec's, which is why this is LOW. | `identityPalette.ts` (spec section 1) |
| 3 | LOW | R-25 (non-text) | **In light, every identity colour is under 3:1 against the card (1.9 to 3.0).** No meaning is lost: a dot always sits beside its name, and a wallet bar segment beside its wallet row and percentage. It is listed because the Dashboard's wallet bar is the one place where the colours are the only link between segment and row. | Dashboard wallet bar, chips |
| 4 | LOW | copywriting | **"Current color, from before the new palette" uses our word, not the user's.** Since this phase it shows only on a custom category whose colour was picked before Phase 62, and "the new palette" means nothing to someone who never saw the old one. | `ColorGrid.tsx` |
