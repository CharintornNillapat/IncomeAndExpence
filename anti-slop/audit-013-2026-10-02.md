# antislop audit 013: FinLife Tracker after Phase 54 (empty starters), 2026-10-02

This is a mode 2 verification pass, run after the phase as in Phases 55 to 63. It does two things:
- it confirms, or not, that Phase 54 (ADR 0040) resolved audit 001 finding 6, which kept R-17, R-38 and C-5 at FAIL (known, deferred) from audit 002 to audit 012;
- it re-audits all six views (Dashboard, Transactions, Wallets, Debt payoff, Daily diary, Categories) and the shared entry form behind Quick Add, against every rule in the framework.

The direction is `docs/design/finlife-redesign-spec.md` and `DESIGN.md` (dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1). Nothing in the findings table has been changed. **Pick the numbers you want fixed.**

**Two names in the request do not exist here.**
- **"Obsidian Slate".** No theme by that name exists in the repo, `DESIGN.md`, the redesign spec or `src/index.css`. The app has one token system with a light and a dark set (slate surfaces, violet as the one brand colour, ADR 0025 and 0027), and this audit checks against that.
- **"R-01 through R-40".** antislop's rules run R-01 to R-38, plus C-1 to C-5. There is no R-39 or R-40, in the skill or in audits 001 to 012. Every one of the 38 rules and 5 principles is graded below.

**Accepted scope, not a finding.** Phase 54 changes new accounts only, by the owner's decision: a guest ledger already stored on a device, and live accounts seeded before Phase 54, keep their old starter balances. The server's starter wallet is named "Checking Account" and the guest's "Main Checking", also on purpose. Neither counts against R-17, R-38 or C-5.
