# antislop audit 007: FinLife Tracker after Phase 58b (editing a transaction), 2026-09-30

This is a mode 2 verification pass, run after the phase as in Phases 55 to 58a. It covers:
- the selected row's edit panel on the Transactions page (spec 6.2);
- the panel's move to inline from `lg`;
- the Dashboard's Recent activity rows, now buttons that open their row;
- the CSV import's icons.

The direction is `docs/design/finlife-redesign-spec.md` and `DESIGN.md`. One problem was found during the pass and fixed before this report; it is listed below the findings. Nothing in the findings table has been changed. **Pick the numbers you want fixed.**

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding | Where |

| 1 | MEDIUM | C-4, layoutmobile | **At 1024px the desktop tab bar scrolls sideways and cuts "Categories" to "Cate"**.
