# antislop audit 004: FinLife Tracker after Phase 56 (shared components), 2026-09-28

This is a mode 2 verification pass, run after the phase as in Phase 55. It covers what Phase 56 changed:
- the fourteen shared components of spec section 4;
- where the drop-ins were adopted;
- the one global focus style.

The direction is `docs/design/finlife-redesign-spec.md`, and `DESIGN.md` records its rules and deviations. Nothing below has been fixed. **Pick the numbers you want fixed.**

## How it was checked

- **Code sweeps** of the Phase 56 diff (`main..HEAD`, `src/`):
  - em dashes in added user-facing strings: 0;
  - a `focus:outline-none` with no replacement: 0;
  - new blur, pulse, scale, spring or entrance animation: 0. `OverflowMenu` opens without animation, and `ProgressBar`'s tween dropped from 500ms to 200ms;
  - raw palette classes: 0;
  - hand-typed primary, secondary or outline-danger button strings left outside `ui/Button`: 1, the mobile centre Quick Add, a listed exception.
- **Contrast.** `node scripts/wcag-tokens.mjs` passes every pair in both themes, including the new ones:
  - `chip-text` on the adjust tint;
  - the focus outline on the header and on `surface-3`;
  - expense text on the menu surfaces.
- **Browser.** The Playwright MCP opened every view as a guest, with one transaction seeded:
  - at 1280px, light and dark: all six views;
  - at 390px, dark: four views.

  An audit script listed every button, link and field and flagged three things: any under 44px, any whose 4px focus ring an ancestor would clip, and horizontal page overflow. Real Tab presses checked the outline's computed style.
- **Tests.**
  - Lint clean.
  - Unit 353/353.
  - Playwright: four full local runs, the last two 357/357. Two earlier runs had intermittent timeouts; see finding 3.
