# antislop audit 001: FinLife Tracker (2026-09-27)

Mode 2 (after the fact). Nothing in the app has been changed. Pick the numbers you want fixed; any number you do not name stays as it is.

**Scope.** Every screen in `src/` (47 `.tsx` files), at commit `2dce5d0`.

**Direction.** There is no `DESIGN.md`, and you chose to skip direction for now. So this audit judges the app against the rules only, not against a brand, and it sets no energy, rhythm or motion levels.

**Priority** follows antislop's tiers:
- **HIGH:** a Hard Gate rule, which has no exceptions.
- **MEDIUM:** a Purpose-Gate rule. The technique is allowed, but only with a written reason.
- **LOW:** a Quality Lock, a consistency rule.

## How it was checked

- **Code sweep.** Every rule that shows up in source was searched for: em dashes, buzzwords, removed focus outlines, blur, glow, gradients, animation, uppercase labels, emoji and icon imports.
- **Contrast.** I used antislop's WCAG 2.x formula, run in Node because Python is not installed here. It reproduces the skill's reference table exactly (4.48, 2.82, 5.74).
  - The colours are the ones Tailwind v4 actually compiled into `dist/assets/index-*.css` (OKLCH converted to sRGB), not the older published hex values.
- **Measured in a real browser.** Each of the six views was opened in Chromium at a 390 px phone width, with touch enabled, in both light and dark mode. The script recorded:
  - every control smaller than 44 px;
  - horizontal overflow;
  - console errors.
- **Not covered by the browser pass:**
  - the signed-in screens, because the pass ran as a guest;
  - the inside of most modals;
  - transaction rows, because a fresh guest has no transactions.

## Findings

### HIGH (Hard Gate)

**1. Grey secondary text is unreadable in light mode (R-25).**
- `text-stone-400` measures **2.59:1** on white and **2.48:1** on `stone-50`. The minimum is 4.5:1 for normal text, and even the 3:1 large-text bar is missed.
- It is used **86 times in 27 files**: captions, helper text, table metadata, empty-state lines and icons.
- `text-stone-300` used as text measures **1.49:1** (8 uses in 7 files, mostly placeholders).
- `text-stone-500` passes, at 4.81:1 on white.

**2. Grey secondary text fails in dark mode too (R-25).**
- `dark:text-stone-500` measures **3.64:1** on `stone-900` (62 uses in 22 files), which passes only for large text.
- `dark:text-stone-600` measures **2.29:1** (7 uses in 6 files).
- `stone-400` on `stone-900` passes, at 6.76:1.

**3. The brand green and amber are too light for text (R-25).**
- `text-emerald-600` on white measures **3.67:1** (27 uses in 16 files). It colours income amounts and success messages.
- White text on `emerald-600` also measures **3.67:1**. That affects dark mode's primary buttons, including the mobile centre Quick Add, and 2 light-mode uses.
- `text-amber-600` measures **3.19:1** (9 uses in 8 files).
- `rose-600` passes, at 4.51:1.

**4. Controls under the 44 px tap minimum on a phone (R-03).** These were measured, not estimated:

| Top bar | `#navbar-sync-badge-btn` | 57×21 |

| Dashboard | `#insights-collapse-btn` | 26×26 |
| Wallets | `#wallet-transfer-modal-btn`, `#wallet-add-modal-btn` | 32 tall |

**5. A focus outline is removed with nothing in its place (R-32).**
- In `WalletPopupModal.tsx:409` the wallet `<select>` has `focus:outline-none` and no replacement, so keyboard users cannot see when they are on it.
- The only other outline removal, the amount field in `InlineMathInput`, replaces it properly with a border and ring on its container. That one passes.
- Buttons with no focus classes keep the browser's default outline. That also passes.

**6. Starter money nobody entered (R-38, R-17).**
- **Guests** see Main Checking ฿2,500, Cash Wallet ฿150, Savings Reserve ฿5,000 and a Student Loan with ฿4,500 left of ฿10,000. Nothing labels any of it as sample data.
- **A new signed-in account** is seeded in the real database with ฿10,650 across three wallets (Checking Account ฿2,500, Cash Wallet ฿150, Savings Reserve ฿8,000). None of it has a ledger row.
- **Fixing this needs a decision first.** `CLAUDE.md` exempts the starter wallets from opening-balance rows, and `tests/helpers.ts` plus several specs rely on a fresh context having these wallets and the debt.

**7. A failed cloud sync is invisible (R-27).**
- When `loadSupabaseData` cannot read a table, it only writes `console.error('[Supabase Sync Error] ...')`.
- A signed-in user then sees stale or partial numbers with nothing on screen saying the load failed or how to retry.
- The loading and empty states exist. This is the missing error state.

**8. Em dashes in text users see (R-02).**
- `App.tsx:317`, the footer: "FinLife Tracker — Full-Stack ...".
- `TransactionsView.tsx:284`: "Jev is unavailable right now — import still works ...".
- `TransactionsView.tsx:310`: "Classified N of M — N applied ...".
- `TransactionTableRow.tsx:92` shows "—" alone as a "no category" marker.
- **Related:** `AccountModal.tsx:172` uses a spaced hyphen as a dash ("... on this device - they are not synced ..."). I wrote that line in Phase 52.

**9. No recorded design direction (R-37).**
- The app has no `DESIGN.md`, so no stated identity, palette reasoning or energy, rhythm and motion levels.
- You chose to skip this for now. It is listed because it is a Hard Gate rule, and because findings 10 to 15 are hard to settle without it.
- The fix is yours to author: a `DESIGN.md`, or answers I transcribe into one.

### MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

**10. Decorative colour orbs in the dashboard hero (R-01).** `TotalWealthHero.tsx:25-26` puts two blurred circles behind the balance, one emerald and one indigo.
- The indigo appears nowhere else as decoration; elsewhere it means "transfer or adjustment".
- The hero also has `backdrop-blur-sm` over its own solid background, where the blur has nothing to blur.

**11. A status dot that marks no status (R-13, R-19, R-31).** `TotalWealthHero.tsx:31` shows a green dot pulsing forever beside "Total Money Across All Wallets". It marks no live or changing state.
- Not included here: the mic's pulse, which is honest because it runs only while recording.

**12. Stacked loading animation (R-19).** `ViewLoadingFallback` runs a pulsing square, a spinning ring and three bouncing dots at once, over the words "Loading Module". One indicator would do the job.

**13. Glass on more than two surfaces (R-10).**
- `backdrop-blur` is on the mobile bottom nav, the modal overlay and the modal panel, the wallet popup overlay, and the dashboard hero.
- The cap is one or two, each with a reason. The bottom nav's blur is the one with an obvious reason: content scrolls under it.

**14. Uppercase labels with wide letter-spacing (R-06).**
- These appear in 13 files: "Total Money Across All Wallets", "Current Balance", "Balance", "Day Outflow", "Loading Module", "Select CSV File" and others.
- They are allowed only with a written reason. None exists.

**15. One default icon library everywhere (R-04).**
- Every icon comes from `lucide-react` (31 files). The icons themselves are relevant to their content; the tell is that the set was a default rather than a choice.
- Keeping it with a one-line written reason in a future `DESIGN.md` would close this finding without changing a pixel.

## What passed

- **No horizontal overflow** on any view at 390 px, in either theme.
- **No console errors** while clicking through all six views, in either theme.
- **No gradients or neon colours, and no background grids.** The glow on the centre Quick Add button marks its elevation and is the only one.
- **No fake testimonials, no invented user or uptime statistics, no fabricated trust or security claims** (apart from finding 6's sample balances).
- **Every nav item reaches a real view.** The mobile More sheet reaches Debts, Diary, Categories and Account.
- **Emoji are used on purpose.** The five diary faces are the mood scale itself. ✓ appears on three success messages, and → marks transfer direction. Neither is decoration.
- **Shadows mark real elevation:** modals, the update toast and the centre Quick Add button.
- **Motion is mostly honest.** The spinners run during real work, the mic pulses only while recording, and the insight skeleton holds the space its sentences will fill.
- **Empty states name the next step,** for example "Add a wallet to start tracking balances, transfers, and transactions".
