# antislop audit 011: FinLife Tracker after Phase 62 (the Categories page), 2026-10-01

This is a mode 2 verification pass, run after the phase as in Phases 55 to 61. It covers:
- the rebuilt Categories page (spec 6.6): the header and its tablist, the grouped list, the New and Edit form, the colour grid (L9) and the locked System group (L10);
- Add, Edit, Cancel and Delete on that page, and the edit sheet below `lg`;
- the Smart rules tab, which was moved and recopied but not redesigned (spec 11);
- the header's tab bar, now icon-only from 768 to 1279px.

The direction is `docs/design/finlife-redesign-spec.md` and `DESIGN.md`. Nothing in the findings table has been changed. **Pick the numbers you want fixed.**

## How it was checked

- **Code sweeps** of the Phase 62 diff (`43524c4..HEAD`, `src/`):
  - em dashes in added user-facing strings: 0;
  - raw palette classes: 0;
  - `focus:outline-none`: 0;
  - `transition-colors` / `transition-all`: 0;
  - motion added: 0;
  - `font-mono`: 0;
  - uppercase: 1, the rules table's column headers, which `DESIGN.md` allows.

  Before the phase:
  - the raw type enum was printed on every row;
  - the rows carried uppercase "Default" and "In use" tags;
  - each row had a pencil and a trash icon;
  - the sandbox had a sparkle icon;
  - the picker offered the money colours.

  All of that is gone. A unit test asserts that no raw type reaches the page.
- **Contrast.** No token was added. `node scripts/wcag-tokens.mjs` passes.
- **Browser.** Playwright's own Chromium was driven by a script against the dev server, as a guest.
  - Seed: the nine shipped categories plus four custom ones: one in use by a transaction, one with a 58-character name, one named in Thai, and one income.
  - Viewports: 1280px light, 1024px light, 900px dark, and 390px light and dark.
  - Every control in `main` was checked for a hit box under 44px, the page for horizontal overflow, and the header's tab bar for its own overflow.
  - Driven for real, all with the keyboard:
    - Enter on a row opens it and focus moves to the form's heading;
    - Cancel returns focus to the row;
    - Escape on the delete dialog returns focus to Delete;
    - a confirmed delete moves focus to the list's heading and frees the colour;
    - the sheet at 390, and Escape out of it.
  - No console errors at any width.
- **Tests.**
  - Lint is clean, and unit tests pass 560/560.
  - Playwright passed 401/402 in each of two runs. Each run's single failure was an intermittent outside this phase; see the refactor log.

## Findings

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MEDIUM | R-25 (non-text), human | **A used colour reads as a different colour rather than an unavailable one.** At the spec's 0.25 opacity, in dark the used Rose, Aqua, Khaki and Steel become plum, dark teal, brown-grey and slate blobs. In light they become pastels. Nothing but opacity marks them, so the grid looks like twelve colours of which some are just muted. The button's name says "Rose, used by Pets", and the colour cannot be picked, but sighted users get no sign until they try. | `ColorGrid.tsx` |
| 2 | MEDIUM | layoutmobile, C-3 | **At 390px "New category" sits below the whole list.** With the shipped nine plus four, that is about 900px of scrolling past rows to reach Add. Spec 8 has forms open as bottom sheets on small screens. The edit form does; the new one does not. | `CategoriesView.tsx` |
| 3 | LOW | human | **Below 1280px the header's tabs are icons only, and two of the icons don't say what they open.** A falling line for Debt payoff and a book for Daily diary are not obvious, and a tooltip needs a mouse. At 1024 the bar has room to spare: about 300px stand empty between the last tab and the sync badge. | `Navbar.tsx` |
| 4 | LOW | layoutmobile | **In the 390 edit sheet, "Income category" sits tight under the sheet's header rule**, closer than any other gap in the form. | `CategoryForm.tsx` (`-mt-2`) |
| 5 | LOW | C-1 | **"Default" is on seven of the eleven editable rows**, so the tag that should mark the exception marks the rule. Spec 6.6 asks for it, which is why this is LOW. | `CategoryList.tsx` |
