# antislop audit 008: FinLife Tracker after Phase 59 (the Wallets page), 2026-10-01

This is a mode 2 verification pass, run after the phase as in Phases 55 to 58b. It covers:
- the rebuilt Wallets page (spec 6.3): the header, the list, the selected wallet's detail, and the sheet below `lg`;
- Edit, Archive, Unarchive, Delete and Adjust balance on that page;
- the Dashboard's wallet rows, which now open the page instead of the retired popup.

The direction is `docs/design/finlife-redesign-spec.md` and `DESIGN.md`. One bug was found during the pass and fixed before this report; it is listed below the findings. Nothing in the findings table has been changed. **Pick the numbers you want fixed.**

## How it was checked

- **Code sweeps** of the Phase 59 diff (`main..HEAD`, `src/`):
  - em dashes in added user-facing strings: 0;
  - raw palette classes: 0;
  - `focus:outline-none` without a replacement ring: 0 (the one added, on the adjust input, keeps `focus:ring-2 ring-focus`);
  - `transition-colors` / `transition-all`: 0;
  - motion added: 0;
  - `font-mono`, uppercase labels, sparkle icons: 0.
- **Contrast.** No token changed. `node scripts/wcag-tokens.mjs` passes every pair.
- **Browser.** The Playwright MCP server did not connect this session, so Playwright's own Chromium was driven by a script against the dev server, as a guest, seeded with a transfer with a note, an expense with a Thai note, a cancelling adjustment pair and an income.
  - Viewports: 1280px light, 1024px light, 900px dark, 390px light and dark.
  - Every control was checked for a hit box under 44px, and the page for horizontal overflow.
  - Driven for real:
    - selecting a wallet with the mouse and with Enter;
    - Tab order: list rows, dashed Add wallet, Edit, the menu, Transfer out, Adjust balance, View all;
    - the menu by ArrowDown, and Escape, at 1280 and inside the sheet at 390;
    - Archive end to end on a phone;
    - Edit with a blank name (Save disabled);
    - Adjust balance and Edit open together (opening one closes the other).
  - No console errors at either width.
- **Tests.** Lint clean. Unit 490/490 after the fix below (489/489 before it). Playwright 375/375 in both full runs, and the 90 runs of every menu-using spec after the fix.

## Findings

| # | Priority | Rule | Finding | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MEDIUM | R-32 | **Closing a sheet drops keyboard focus to the page.** After the wallet sheet closes (Escape, the close button or the scrim), focus lands on `<body>`, not on the wallet row that opened it, so a keyboard user starts again from the top. This is `Modal`'s behaviour everywhere in the app (the Transactions panel's sheet too), not new in this phase, but this page opens a sheet on every wallet tap below `lg`. | `Modal.tsx` |
| 2 | MEDIUM | spec 3, R-29 | **Edit offers a red and a near-black wallet colour.** The swatches are `AddWalletForm`'s palette (`#ef4444`, `#0f172a` among eight), and spec section 3 keeps red off wallets. Edit is a second place to pick one. The palette itself waits for spec 5.1's colour migration. | `walletFormStyles.ts` |
| 3 | LOW | R-32, human | **The colour swatches are named by hex code** ("Colour #0284c7"), which a screen reader reads out digit by digit. Same as `AddWalletForm`. | `WalletDetail.tsx`, `AddWalletForm.tsx` |
| 4 | LOW | C-2 | **An archived wallet can only be unarchived.** Its row has no way to see its activity or delete it; a person has to unarchive it first. The Transactions page's wallet filter still finds its rows. | `WalletList.tsx` |
| 5 | LOW | C-4, layoutmobile | **Audit 007 finding 1 is still visible at 1024px:** the header's tab bar cuts "Categories" to "Cate". The owner deferred it to the Categories redesign; it is listed because this page is now master-detail at that width. | `Navbar.tsx` |

**Fixed during the pass:**
- **Escape in the "⋯" menu inside the sheet closed the menu and the whole sheet**, and dropped focus. The menu handled Escape, but the event still reached `Modal`'s `document` listener. This phase is the first to put an `OverflowMenu` inside a `Modal`. The menu now stops its own Escape. A unit test with a `document` listener failed before the fix and passes after it, and the browser check shows the sheet staying open, with focus on the menu button.

**Found by the walk-through and fixed before the gate** (see the refactor log): the balance box had no inner padding; a 4/12 list cut wallet names off at 1024; the balance buttons wrapped into a column beside the figure.

**Still open, deferred by the owner:**
- Audit 001 finding 6 (the starter money), to Phase 54.
- The sparkle icons in `InlineMathInput`, `TransactionForm`, `CategorySuggestionChip` and `CategoriesView`, each with its own page phase (audit 005).
- Audit 004 finding 1 (the raw enum in the Categories list), and audit 007 findings 1 and 4, to the Categories page.
