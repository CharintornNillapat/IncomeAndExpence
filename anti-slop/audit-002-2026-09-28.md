## How it was checked

- **Code sweeps** of `src/` for:
  - raw palette classes: 0 left;
  - `whileHover` / `whileTap`: 0;
  - `backdrop-blur`: 2, the mobile nav and the CSV preview's sticky header;
  - `animate-pulse` / `bounce` / `ping`: the microphone only, while recording;
  - em dashes in rendered text;
  - `focus:outline-none` without a replacement.
- **Contrast.**
  - `node scripts/wcag-tokens.mjs` passes every token pair in both themes.
  - The compiled `dist/assets/index-*.css` carries the same token values, spot-checked for `--fg-muted`, `--line-input` and `--pending-text` in each theme.
- **Browser.** The Playwright MCP opened all six views as a guest in Chromium at 390 px and recorded:
  - every visible `button`, link, `input`, `select`, `textarea` and `[role=button]` under 44 px on either axis;
  - every button with no accessible name;
  - horizontal overflow;
  - console errors.
  It then opened the transfer modal at 1280 px in dark mode, loaded the Debts tab cold to watch the skeleton, and moved keyboard focus onto a transfer `<select>`.
- **All three engines.** The project's own Playwright rendered the hero balance in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit, and screenshotted it.
- **Not covered:**
  - the signed-in screens, because no browser pass signs in; the sync states are covered by five unit tests instead;
  - the CSV preview's per-row controls, which need a file loaded;
  - category chips, which take each category's own colour and were not re-measured.
