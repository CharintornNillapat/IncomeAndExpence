# antislop audit 006: FinLife Tracker after Phase 58a (the Transactions page), 2026-09-30

This is a mode 2 verification pass, run after the phase as in Phases 55 to 57. It covers the Transactions page rebuilt to spec 6.2:
- the header and its "Import / export" menu;
- the filter row;
- the day-grouped list with "Load 25 more";
- the selected row's panel, which holds Delete and Restore (editing is Phase 58b).

The direction is `docs/design/finlife-redesign-spec.md` and `DESIGN.md`. Three problems were found during the pass and fixed before this report, and are listed below the findings. Nothing in the findings table has been changed. **Pick the numbers you want fixed.**

## How it was checked

- **Code sweeps** of the Phase 58a diff (`main..HEAD`, `src/`):
  - em dashes in added user-facing strings: 0;
  - raw palette classes: 0;
  - `focus:outline-none` without a ring: 0;
  - `transition-colors`: 0;
  - motion: 0 new. The one `backdrop-blur` hit is the import preview's sticky table header, one of DESIGN.md's two allowed blurs, which moved unchanged into `ImportCsvModal`.
- **Contrast.** No token changed. `node scripts/wcag-tokens.mjs` still passes every pair.
- **Browser.** The Playwright MCP opened the page as a guest, seeded with 33 rows: 28 expenses over ten days, an income, a transfer with a note, a cancelling adjustment pair and a deleted row.
  - Viewports: 1280px light, 900px dark and 390px light, with and without a row selected.
  - An audit script checked every control for a hit box under 44px, a clipped 4px focus ring, and page overflow.
  - Each filter, "Load more", Delete, Show deleted, Restore, Escape and the focus hand-off were driven for real.
- **Tests.** Lint clean. Unit 396/396. Playwright two full runs, 356/357 each (finding 3).

**Fixed during the pass** (`3b639ca`):
- **Clipped focus rings.** The list card's `overflow-hidden` clipped every row's focus ring. The list is now inset 8px with rounded rows.
- **The type control's "All" was 43px wide.** `SegmentedControl` options keep a 44px minimum width now, which also covers the other pages' controls.
- **The phone filter row wrapped unevenly.** The selects now share their row.

**Closed by this phase:** audit 004 finding 4. The table that was 16px wider than its scroller at 390px is gone, and the page has no overflow at 390px.

**Also found:** Chromium's full-page screenshot briefly reports a narrower viewport. That flipped the panel into its dialog in one capture. The DOM, the plain screenshots and the Playwright suite at 1280px show one panel, as designed (recorded in ADR `0031`).
