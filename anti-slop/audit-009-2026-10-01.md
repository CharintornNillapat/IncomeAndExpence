# antislop audit 009: FinLife Tracker after Phase 60 (the Debt payoff page), 2026-10-01

This is a mode 2 verification pass, run after the phase as in Phases 55 to 59. It covers:
- the rebuilt Debt payoff page (spec 6.4): the header, the summary, the debt cards and the "Paid off (N)" section;
- Edit, Mark as paid off and Delete, and the unchanged Make repayment, on that page.

The direction is `docs/design/finlife-redesign-spec.md` and `DESIGN.md`. No bug was found during the pass. Nothing in the findings table has been changed. **Pick the numbers you want fixed.**

## How it was checked

- **Code sweeps** of the Phase 60 diff (`main..HEAD`, `src/`):
  - em dashes in added user-facing strings: 0;
  - raw palette classes: 0;
  - `focus:outline-none`: 0;
  - `transition-colors` / `transition-all`: 0;
  - motion added: 0;
  - `font-mono` and sparkle icons: 0;
  - uppercase: one, the summary's three compact metric labels (Still owed, Paid off), which DESIGN.md allows.
- **Contrast.** No token changed. `node scripts/wcag-tokens.mjs` passes every pair, including the ones the new tags use: `pending-body` on `pending-bg`, `expense-text` on `expense-bg`, `income-text` on `surface-2`, and `fg` and `fg-secondary` on `surface-3`.
- **Browser.** The Playwright MCP server did not connect this session, so Playwright's own Chromium was driven by a script against the dev server, as a guest. The ledger was seeded with five debts:
  - a dated debt with interest (Student Loan);
  - a long name and a ฿214,500.50 remainder;
  - an overdue, interest-free debt;
  - an undated debt;
  - a paid-off debt.

  The checks:
  - Viewports: 1280px light, 1024px light, 900px dark, 390px light and dark.
  - Every control in `main` was checked for a hit box under 44px, and the page for horizontal overflow.
  - Driven for real:
    - the ⋯ menu by keyboard (ArrowDown into Edit, ArrowDown to Delete, Escape back to the trigger);
    - Edit opened from the menu with Enter, then closed with Escape (focus back on ⋯);
    - Mark as paid off: Escape (focus back on the button), then confirm (the debt moved to Paid off, and focus went to its ⋯);
    - the repay modal opening from a card.
  - No console errors at any width.
- **Tests.** Lint clean. Unit 516/516. Playwright: four full runs, 384, 383, 383 and 384 of 384. The two failures were Firefox timeouts in the mobile-nav block, which this phase does not touch: the file's known intermittent. Targeted repeats then passed 240/240 (see the refactor log).
