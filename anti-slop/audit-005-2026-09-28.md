# antislop audit 005: FinLife Tracker after Phase 57 (the Dashboard), 2026-09-28

This is a mode 2 verification pass, run after the phase as in Phases 55 and 56. It covers what Phase 57 changed:
- the Dashboard, rebuilt to spec 6.1;
- the `main` padding;
- the `transition-control` sweep that closes audit 004 finding 2.

The direction is `docs/design/finlife-redesign-spec.md` and its mockup, and `DESIGN.md` records the rules and the deviations. Three problems were found during the pass and fixed before this report (listed under "Fixed during the pass"). Nothing below the findings table has been changed. **Pick the numbers you want fixed.**

## How it was checked

- **Code sweeps** of the Phase 57 diff (`main..HEAD`, `src/`):
  - em dashes in added user-facing strings: 0. The Net worth caption "Wallets − debt" uses the minus sign U+2212;
  - raw palette classes: 0;
  - new `focus:outline-none`: 0. The 10 hits in the diff are existing fields whose line changed only in the transition sweep, and each keeps its `focus:ring-2`;
  - new blur, glow, pulse, scale, spring or entrance animation: 0. The one `shadow-` hit is the mobile Quick Add's existing glow, again touched only by the sweep;
  - `transition-colors` left in `src/`: 0.
- **Contrast.** `node scripts/wcag-tokens.mjs` passes every pair in both themes, including the two new ones:
  - `fg` on the warning tint (the banner's figures): 17.22 and 14.05;
  - the mood meter's filled bars against its empty ones: 4.79 and 5.72, against a 3:1 minimum for graphics.
- **Browser.** The Playwright MCP opened the Dashboard as a guest, seeded with an income, three expenses, a transfer, a cancelling adjustment pair, two diary days and two debts with due dates.
  - Viewports: 1280px light, 900px dark and 390px light.
  - An audit script listed every control in `main` and flagged any under 44px, any whose 4px focus ring an ancestor would clip, and any page overflow.
  - The four periods were switched in turn, reading the Cash flow Spending and the category total each time.
  - The focus outline's computed colour was read straight after focus, on five control types.
- **Tests.**
  - Lint clean. Unit 385/385.
  - Playwright: three full local runs, 353, 356 and 356 of 357. Every failure was a timeout, never an assertion (see finding 3).
